30th June 2026
Accounting API: We’ve added ULTRA to the Class enum returned by the Organisation endpoint to cater for Australian organisations using the new Xero Ultra plan.
If your integration handles organisation plan types, update it to support ULTRA. Integrations that assume only existing values may fail to maintain their connection to Xero.
