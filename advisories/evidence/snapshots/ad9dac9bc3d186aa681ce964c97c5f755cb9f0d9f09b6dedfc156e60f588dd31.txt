10 September 2026 by Xero Developer Relations
The IsQualifyingEarnings property is now required in all create and update requests for AU Earnings Rates and Pay Items. Requests that omit it will return a 400 validation error.
If IsQualifyingEarnings is true: then Pay Items: IsExemptFromSuper must be false and Earnings Rates: IsSubjectToSuper must be true.
