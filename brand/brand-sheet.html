SHAPI brand sheet

A square cut in two by one curve: your code on the left, the API on the right, two pieces that still fit. Beside it, the name in Inter SemiBold with a bar under api: the API side, held. That is the product in one picture. SHAPI finds where an API's shape stopped fitting your code, and keeps it in place with a tested fix.

Colour

Ink#0E0F12
Paper#F7F6F2

One dark, one light, and nothing else. The mark also works in pure black and pure white. No accent colour, no gradients.

Type

The wordmark is Inter SemiBold, lowercase, letter-spacing −1.5%, converted to outlines with Inter's own kerning so it never depends on an installed font. Inter is free under the SIL Open Font License; the licence is in brand/fonts/Inter-OFL.txt. Use Inter for any text set beside the logo.

Construction

Every shape is stored as numbers in brand/build_logo.mjs: the square, the curve, the cut width, the font size, the bar's position (12 units below the baseline, 6 units tall, from the a to the end of the i), and the gap between mark and word (16 units).

In the horizontal lockup, the mark is exactly as tall as the h: its top meets the ascender and its foot sits on the baseline.

Optical corrections: below 48 px the mark is redrawn on the pixel grid (see Small sizes), because a scaled cut blurs shut.

Lockups

Ink artwork on Paper, Paper artwork on Ink. Files: brand/svg/ and brand/png/ (512, 1024 and 2048 px, transparent and on background).

Clear space and minimum size

Smallest sizes:

Horizontal lockup: at least 20 px tall (about 80 px wide). Mark alone: 16 px, using the hand-drawn version.

Small sizes

At 16, 32 and 48 px the mark is drawn on the pixel grid: the cut is snapped to whole pixels row by row, so it stays open and sharp. Next to each, the vector scaled down, for comparison.

Files: brand/favicon/ (favicon-16/32/48.png, favicon.ico, favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-512.png). The SVG favicon switches to Paper when the browser is dark.

Motion

"Join": your code slides in from the left, the API drops in from above and locks with a small settle, the word fades in, and the bar draws under api. About 1.6 seconds, once, and it ends on the static logo. Plain CSS, no script: brand/motion/shapi-motion.css with the markup in brand/svg/web/shapi-lockup-animatable.svg.

Don'ts

Do: Ink on Paper or Paper on Ink, half a mark of clear space, the hand-drawn mark below 48 px, and the static logo wherever motion would distract.
